## BIP39 English dictionary

`bip39-english.txt` is the public 2,048-word BIP39 dictionary, extracted from
Pocket Node's pinned `Bip39EnglishWordlist.kt`. Its bytes match
[bitcoin/bips at revision `927b6de9915c9262615a6399de51b200f81e5aa4`](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/927b6de9915c9262615a6399de51b200f81e5aa4/bip-0039/english.txt).
