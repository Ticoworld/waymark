## English BIP39 reference dictionary

`bip39-english.txt` contains the public 2,048-word BIP39 English dictionary,
copied from Pocket Node's pinned `Bip39EnglishWordlist.kt`. Pocket's header names
bitcoin/bips as the source and states it is byte-identical to trezor/python-mnemonic.
The standalone bytes match bitcoin/bips at revision
`927b6de9915c9262615a6399de51b200f81e5aa4`:

- [Dictionary](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/927b6de9915c9262615a6399de51b200f81e5aa4/bip-0039/english.txt)
- [BIP39 specification and MIT declaration](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/927b6de9915c9262615a6399de51b200f81e5aa4/bip-0039.mediawiki)
